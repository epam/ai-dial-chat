Slicing strategy: **risk-first**. The riskiest, most novel piece is the Hourly cron shape (`{ hour: '*', minute: '0' }`) round-tripping correctly through both the forward mapping (`buildCronFields`) and the reverse mapping (`parseCronFields`) without a backend change — that is proven first (Section 2) before any UI wiring depends on it. The `ScheduledTaskRepeat` enum (Section 1) is the shared contract both the lib and the utils slices need, so it lands first as a small, independently-verifiable foundation. UI wiring (Sections 3–5) and i18n (Section 6) build on the proven mapping. Each section ends with its own `nx` verification run.
