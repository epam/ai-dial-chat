# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Restrained connected elf poses and directed hops

The existing scene-local Lottie composition SHALL preserve both elves' recognizable designs, distinct expressions and compact arm connections while refining their proportions and broad shading. Secondary cap rotation SHALL stay within 10 degrees of its authored rest orientation, preserving the brim/head overlap. The bound master's hops SHALL show preparation, decelerating ascent and accelerating descent with horizontal travel continuing through the apex. Ribbon grips, reel and coils SHALL remain synchronized with the original shared winding geometry in LTR and RTL.

The existing provider/session, host anchors, full mobile story, reduced-motion still, restoration, 16-second duration and resource ceilings SHALL remain unchanged: two elves, one player, <=12 layers, <=500 SVG descendants, <=180 paths, <=240 KB composition, <=1,400 total keys, zero particles and zero snapshots. No new public state, API, labels, flags, telemetry or persistence SHALL be introduced. Shared playback/timeline code SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: A pull causes the master to recoil

- **WHEN** the ribbon tightens and the master's head and body react
- **THEN** the cap remains seated on his head and the revised silhouette stays recognizable
- **AND** the helper's hand remains on the ribbon

#### Scenario: The bound master hops away

- **WHEN** a frustrated hop begins
- **THEN** a short grounded preparation precedes lift, the ascent slows toward the apex and descent accelerates toward landing
- **AND** horizontal movement continues through the apex without an extra pause

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Elf artwork and staging support the same readable action
Compiled and pack versions SHALL use consistent revised elf proportions, face contours and broad shading. Their working hands, ribbon and facial reactions SHALL remain distinguishable from nearby interface text at mobile and desktop sizes. The composer remains the measured support; the existing story, grip, outline, lifecycle and resource contracts SHALL remain intact.

#### Scenario: Pull and reaction
- **WHEN** the helper pulls the loose ribbon and the master becomes bound
- **THEN** both faces and the working ribbon remain visually distinct and the angry-master/proud-helper contrast is preserved

#### Scenario: Versioned external artwork
- **WHEN** revised artwork is supplied in a compatible new pack version
- **THEN** published older asset URLs remain unchanged and new hashes, sizes, posters and variants agree with their manifest
