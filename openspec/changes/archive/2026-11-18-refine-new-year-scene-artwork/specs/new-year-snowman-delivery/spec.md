# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Snow volume and lodged head remain readable
The snowman's shadows SHALL follow the rounded body and head contours. When the head is lodged in the fir, the visible face SHALL remain separated from covering needles and the supporting bough at mobile scene size. The round-body design, scarf, mittens, story, branch/head contacts and existing budgets SHALL remain intact.

#### Scenario: Head in the branch
- **WHEN** the head lands in the lower branch and reacts before the sneeze
- **THEN** the face and carrot remain visible above the supporting foliage without moving the established branch socket
