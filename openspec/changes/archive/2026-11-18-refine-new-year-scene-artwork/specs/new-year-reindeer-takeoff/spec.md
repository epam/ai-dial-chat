# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reindeer artwork reads as connected body masses
The adult and calf SHALL retain coherent chest, neck and head contours in bracing, reaching and flight poses. Shading SHALL follow those masses without outlining their overlap as detached pieces. The calf's face SHALL remain readable at mobile scene size. The existing contact, plot, timing and resource contracts SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Bracing and freeing the word
- **WHEN** the adult braces and the calf reaches the caught word at mobile or desktop scene size
- **THEN** their body contours remain connected and the calf's face and word contact remain distinguishable

#### Scenario: Static artwork
- **WHEN** reduced motion selects the static resolution
- **THEN** it uses the revised character design with viewport clearance around the complete cast
