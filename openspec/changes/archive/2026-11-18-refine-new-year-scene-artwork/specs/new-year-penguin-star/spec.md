# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Purposeful walking and continuous star flight

Entrance, approach to the fir and departure SHALL each include alternating short foot lifts coordinated with body travel. During held acting poses the feet SHALL settle. The star SHALL follow one continuous arc from release to the fir without repeated easing stops at intermediate samples. The existing story, 20-second duration, recognizable monochrome character design and ceilings of 140 SVG descendants, 32 animated properties, 80 keys per property and 120 KB composition data SHALL remain intact.

#### Scenario: The penguin approaches after decorating the fir

- **WHEN** the penguin walks from its throwing position to the fir
- **THEN** its feet alternate rather than remain in a static pose while sliding
- **AND** both feet settle before the bow

#### Scenario: The released star reaches the crown

- **WHEN** the star leaves the flipper
- **THEN** it follows a continuous arc and joins the crown star without a visible position jump

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Penguin working poses retain character and readable form
The penguin SHALL retain sunglasses, monochrome coloring and its Santa hat while showing distinct effort, throw and reaction silhouettes. The beak and head SHALL remain distinguishable on light and dark backgrounds. The tree SHALL use coherent rounded branch masses. Existing grip landmarks, story and resource ceilings SHALL remain intact.

#### Scenario: Capturing and throwing
- **WHEN** the penguin captures paper and throws the star at mobile scene size
- **THEN** body and head poses distinguish effort from release while the flipper grip stays attached
